# Scientific and regulatory context

- ICRP Publication 117, Radiological Protection in Fluoroscopically Guided Procedures Performed Outside the Imaging Department. https://www.icrp.org/publication.asp?id=ICRP+Publication+117
- ICRP Publication 139, Occupational Radiological Protection in Interventional Procedures. https://doi.org/10.1177/0146645317750356
- IAEA SSG-46, Radiation Protection and Safety in Medical Uses of Ionizing Radiation. https://www.iaea.org/publications/11102/radiation-protection-and-safety-in-medical-uses-of-ionizing-radiation
- Kuon et al., Identification of less-irradiating tube angulations in invasive cardiology (2004). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jacc.2004.06.057
- Council Directive 2013/59/Euratom. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2013/59/oj
- Portuguese Decreto-Lei 108/2018, as amended. https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/legislacao-consolidada/decreto-lei/2018-117202808

These sources contextualise protection principles and legal limits. They do not establish the simulator's procedure-specific rates, side factors or universal shielding coefficients. The Kuon citation and endpoint interpretation should be checked against the full article before scientific validation.
